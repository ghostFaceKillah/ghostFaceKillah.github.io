Clearing the plot

Five ways the garden could reward finishing the whole queue for a day, on top of the consistency reward it already gives for showing up. Each mockup is drawn in the app's own styling with sample data, so it reads the way it would on the stats or home screen.

The shared premise: a day is cleared when the daily session ends with nothing due and the day's new-card quota met (or no new cards left to meet it). That moment already exists in the app as the home screen's "All done for today 🎉" state; none of these ideas changes the scheduler.

Blooms only on cleared days idea 1

Today the heatmap grows a random plant on any practiced day, flowers included. Instead: a practiced day grows foliage, and only a cleared day gets to flower or fruit, sitting on a little sun-lit disc. The garden you already have starts telling the difference at a glance.

Today's plot idea 3

A garden bed on the home screen with one cell per card in today's queue. Each graded card plants a sprout; the last one makes the whole bed bloom. The reward is the moment itself: you watch the day's plot fill, and the finished bed is the day's plant shown large. Nothing to collect, nothing to lose.

Garden visitors idea 4

The emoji pool already has bees, butterflies and hedgehogs, scattered at random. Make them earned: a visitor only comes to a cleared day, perched next to that day's plant in the heatmap. A visitors' book on the stats screen collects them, and three rare visitors turn up only on clear-streak milestones.

The orchard tree idea 5

One persistent tree on the stats screen that grows only on cleared days: every cleared day hangs one more fruit in the canopy, and the tree itself moves through sapling, young tree and grown tree stages by the total. Missed days don't hurt it; the tree just waits. A long-term, cumulative reward that the heatmap's 18-week window can't hold.

Rewards
every cleared day, cumulatively; growth stages give medium-term goals without a streak's fragility

natural pairings

1 + 4 are the same change to the heatmap seen twice: blooms on cleared days, and visitors on cleared days. Either alone is coherent; together the pool split is foliage / blooms / visitors.

3 + anything: the plot is the only idea that lives on the home screen before you start, so it complements any of the stats-screen rewards rather than competing with them.

2 vs 5 is the real fork: a streak (fragile, motivating) or a cumulative tree (forgiving, slower). Picking one sets the tone of the whole feature.

recording a cleared day (shared by 1, 2, 4, 5)

When: a daily session ends via finishSession with the queue empty, or refreshStats finds due + new = 0 with reviews today > 0 (so finishing across two sittings still counts). Once earned it stays for the day, even if the cap is raised or a deck enabled afterwards; the alternative, re-evaluating at midnight, would make the reward feel revocable.

Where: days[key].cleared = true locally, next to introduced and reviews. Because days is derived from the review log on every hydration, the flag needs one synced source too. Cheapest option: a marker entry in the month log chunk (grade 0, id day/cleared) that rebuildDaysFromLog and the retention/time readers skip; it rides the existing merge, so two devices can't clobber it. Alternative: a clearedDays list in the settings doc, which is simpler to read but goes through whole-doc pushes.

Idea 3 needs none of this: it is computed live from what the home screen already knows.

Drawn to the SRS app's tokens, light and dark. Sample data is hashed, not real. 🌱
